SECTION 00 · HOW TO READ THIS

A menu assembled by someone who knows the kitchen — not a mandate

Not everything below should be built, and several ideas compete for the same slice of a pharmacist's attention. Two rules govern the whole catalogue. Rule one — sequence: everything assumes the audit's foundation lands first (routing, identity, a Copilot that never does its own arithmetic); a second storey on an uncured foundation is how platforms die. Rule two — safety lock: whatever gets built, the seven clinical severity colours stay locked outside every palette, every theme and every animation; decoration never shares a hue with a verdict. Ideas cite their audit dependency as « gate » where one exists.

The six big swings — three inherited, three new to this edition

BIG SWING 01 · inherited

The compatibility layer

Three quiet fields on every one of the 9,555 records: is the capsule shell gelatin-based and of what origin, does the formulation contain alcohol, can the regimen be adapted to fast safely during Ramadan. Surfaced as one badge per medication, reviewed by a qualified religious-affairs advisor alongside the clinical team — a jurisprudence question as much as a pharmacology one. Every European, American and Gulf platform will fail at this for the Algerian market, because it is not a translation exercise: it requires exactly the local expertise this team already proved with the Ramadan adapter. It generalises one tool into a property of the entire dataset.

NEXTeffort L · data curation + advisory boardgate: none (data work can start now)
BIG SWING 02 · inherited

A Copilot that speaks first

Today the AI only answers. Every data source on the platform already knows things worth surfacing: the Family Cabinet knows an EpiPen expires in nine days; the shortage centre knows a watched drug is back in stock; the Ramadan adapter knows a schedule needs re-timing before Friday; the dataset knows a favourited drug was just withdrawn. A proactive « Pour vous » panel — populated from existing data, requiring no new clinical judgement — turns four built features into one continuously useful colleague instead of four tools a pharmacist must remember to check.

NEXTeffort M · rules over existing storesgate: P1-10 history, recall flags (Wave 1)
BIG SWING 03 · inherited

The counter, not the tab

Barcode scan → Chifa card read → automatic interaction check against everything else on the counter → printed patient handout, as one motion at the point of dispensing. Every piece already exists in isolation (scanner working, Chifa simulator accurate, interactions engine A-grade, print pipeline present); chained, they stop being a reference a pharmacist opens in a spare moment and become the software the counter runs on. A materially different product built entirely from features that already work — plus the high-alert double-check protocol (new below) as its safety spine.

LONG BETeffort XL · flow design + hardware tolerancegate: P0-01 routing, P1-16 Chifa repair
BIG SWING 04 · ★ NEW IN V2

The official-price guardian

A patient-facing page — no account, no login — where anyone types a medication and sees the official PPA price, the Chifa reimbursement rate, and a calm answer to « est-ce normal ? ». One button: « le prix payé était différent » → a moderated report that feeds a public transparency statistic per wilaya. This is the mass-market trust channel the platform has been missing: prices in Algeria are regulated, so DzPharm can be the citizen's verifier without ever accusing anyone — and every verification is an on-ramp to the family cabinet, the garde list and the Copilot. Nothing in the corpus proposes giving the public a reason to open DzPharm; this is it. Virality is built in: the answer screenshot travels through family WhatsApp groups on its own.

BIG SWING 05 · ★ NEW IN V2

The safety net — dispensing-error elimination as a product surface

Three of the world's classic dispensing-error classes, attacked with data the platform already holds: (1) LASA — look-alike/sound-alike pairs (DOPamine/DOBUTamine, CELÉBREX/CERÉZYX…) flagged at search and selection with Tall-Man lettering (« dopaMINE » vs « DOBUtamine »), extended to packaging similarity once the image database exists. (2) High-alert double-check — insulin, methotrexate, anticoagulants and opioids get a mandatory confirm step with the dose restated in words (« vingt unités — 20 UI ») and a printed counter checklist. (3) Duplicate therapy — « DOLIPRANE + DOLIPRANE ADULT = paracetamol ×2 » caught automatically (the corpus's N18) as the basket grows, not after analysis. The audit's deterministic dose engine (P0-08) is the arithmetic backbone. No competitor in the market has any of the three; together they let DzPharm claim the one sentence that matters most in pharmacy: « fewer errors happen where DzPharm is open. »

BIG SWING 06 · ★ NEW IN V2

Self-healing data — the moat that maintains itself

The dataset is the platform, and today it ages silently (prices verified Aug 2026; monographs grew 911→932 mid-audit with no announcement; a withdrawn AMM stays live until a human notices). The self-healing stack: an ANPP/Ministry regulatory-sync bot that crawls official bulletins, diffs against records, flags what went stale and auto-notifies every user who viewed an affected drug; visible freshness (lastVerifiedAt badges everywhere — audit P2-27); a correction-request pipeline where any pharmacist fixes the record and earns credit; weekly price snapshots that quietly build the 24-month history chart's data from today; and the clinical eval harness (200 expert answers, ≥95% forever) that keeps the AI honest as data changes. A dataset that repairs itself is a moat that widens with time instead of draining.

SECTION 01

Design language & visual identity — how the platform should feel

The design constitution, consolidated from The Design Universe and the live design-exploration document, plus new surfaces nobody has touched: sound, daylight, e-ink, and the physical counter. Five principles govern everything below — clinical trust (every pixel communicates competence), Algerian identity (locally rooted, never imported), modern restraint (Linear/Vercel confidence; negative space is the decoration), accessibility first (WCAG is a design constraint that produces better design), and performance as design (a fast page IS a design decision — the 88.8 KB budget is a feature).

Motion that communicates NOWThe 12-interaction motion catalogue as law: card-hover lift 150ms, button press scale(0.97), favourite-star bounce, modal slideUp 300ms on cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), toast slide-in, Copilot thinking-dots, streaming tokens. Fast in, slow out; 150–250ms sweet spot; every animation answers a question (« did my click work? ») or it is cut. prefers-reduced-motion respected absolutely.

The beacon-ping rule NOWThe pulsing emergency-banner dot is reserved for genuine urgency, forever. The moment the same pulse marks a promo badge or a notification, it stops meaning either. Safety animations are a protected species.

Delight rationed is delight remembered. (The first-Copilot-query confetti from the corpus stays optional garnish, reduced-motion gated.)

SECTION 02

The colour universe — 50+ palette directions, one locked law

The palette system is the platform's showpiece: ~400 tokens, 20 named Nuancier palettes with cultural identities, a 7-level clinical state system, AAA contrast on 11 of 12 measured pairs. This section consolidates every palette direction proposed anywhere in the corpus (24 from The Design Universe, 9 from the live exploration, 3 brand-direction swatch sets from the UX Review) and adds the generation machinery that lets it scale to fifty without fifty hand-tunings.

The one law that overrides every palette below (audit P0-07): the seven clinical severity levels live in a locked token set no Nuancier selection, theme or animation can touch, and they always render as colour + text label + icon. Decoration may never share a hue family with a clinical verdict — if a decorative green and the « compatible » badge do the same job on the retina, one of them is lying. The Grenade palette ships only after a side-by-side separation check against locked danger-red; if a pharmacist can't tell them apart in under a second, it shifts toward magenta until they can.

2.1 The formula before the palette

Nine roles, not nine colours

Every palette fills the same hue-less skeleton: canvas · surface · surface-raised · ink-primary · ink-secondary · ink-tertiary · border · accent · accent-soft. Get the roles right and almost any hue becomes coherent — which is exactly how the existing twenty scale.

The lightness-step method

Don't pick nine colours by eye: step one hue through fixed lightness increments. Dark mode — canvas L8–10%, surface L14–16%, raised L20–22%, ink L92%+. Light mode inverts — canvas L96–98%, ink L15–18%. Because every step is a fixed distance from its neighbour, contrast is correct by construction, not by luck — and any future palette is generated by choosing one hue and running the steps.

Combination rules

  • 70 / 25 / 5 — not the living-room 60/30/10: a data-dense clinical table needs ~70% canvas+surface, 25% structural ink+borders, and accent rationed to 5% — one button, one link colour, one focus ring.
  • Contrast hierarchy — priority 1 (body text) gets the highest ratio (14–18:1, AAA); borders get the lowest (non-text, ~1.5:1). Importance is legible before it is read.
  • Gradients — fine on a hero wash; never on body text, never on a small icon, never anywhere near a severity badge. A gradient softens exactly the sharp edge a safety signal needs.
  • Light mode is tuned, not inverted — the light primary (#026AA7 class) is deliberately darker than dark-mode's (#38BDF8) because accents need more saturation on white. Background lifts to #F4F6FA (audit P2-01).

SECTION 03

Symbols & iconography — the DzPharm icon language beyond Lucide

118 Lucide instances across 52 names, zero raster images — an excellent, consistent base. But Lucide is generic: it has no Chifa card, no Algerian pharmacy cross, no AMM seal, no wilaya. The corpus specifies 25 custom symbols (15 + 10); this edition adds 10 more for the new features below, and codifies the style axes so every future icon answers the same three questions the same way.

The style axes (law for everything below)

  • Grid & stroke: 24×24, 2px stroke, round caps and joins, outline-only, 1.5px optical alignment, stroke="currentColor". 2px matches the existing 118 exactly — a mismatched stroke reads as a different family even when shapes agree.
  • Fill treatment: outline = default UI · filled = active/selected · duotone = empty states only (never mixed within a screen region).
  • Size scale: 16 inline · 20 buttons · 24 nav/standalone · 32 feature callouts · 48–88 empty-state illustrations (same DNA, larger stage — no new style invented for illustration).
  • Legibility test: every icon must survive 16px and greyscale; negative space is as designed as the stroke.

Why custom symbols are strategy, not decoration

A Chifa-card icon, a pharmacy cross with the Algerian crescent, a mortar-and-pestle with local geometry — these do two jobs no Lucide icon can: they tell an Algerian pharmacist « this was built for my practice, not translated into it », and they make screenshots of DzPharm instantly recognisable in the WhatsApp groups where the platform's growth actually happens. Symbols are the smallest possible unit of brand.

SECTION 09

Performance & response time — speed is a clinical feature

A pharmacist with a queue and a phone on 4G experiences latency as distrust. The targets, from the audit's KPI table: first Copilot token < 1 s (p95) · search keystroke < 50 ms · medication page < 300 ms at the edge · offline bundle < 500 KB · Lighthouse mobile > 90 on throttled 4G. Every idea below serves one of those five numbers — or measures them honestly (V9's lesson: warm European edge measurements describe nobody in Algiers).

The four refusals — the won't-do list

1 · No advertising on clinical surfaces. Ever. A sponsored result next to an interaction warning converts the platform's only non-renewable asset — trust — into inventory. If revenue is needed, it comes from Pro/institution tiers and B2B data products with explicit governance, never from the space beside a contre-indication.
2 · No sale or licence of patient-identifiable data. Aggregated, anonymised, governance-reviewed statistics (the national dashboard, conditions a–d) or nothing. The DPIA (audit P1-28) is a ceiling, not a starting point.
3 · No unmoderated drug ratings or reviews. Crowdsourced clinical signal (shortages, antibiogram, syrup acceptability) always ships with moderation, verification weighting, sample-size honesty and expiry. A one-star review on a life-saving drug is misinformation with a UI.
4 · No diagnostic claims. Symptom tools stay guidance-plus-red-flags (« when to call 14/102 »), the Copilot keeps its refusal language, and every surface keeps the P1-27 disclaimer. The app-store strategy and the Law 85-05 posture depend on this line holding.

The sequencing laws (repeat them until they are boring)

  1. Foundation before features. Routing, identity, streaming, the dose engine — then everything else. A second storey on an uncured foundation is how platforms die.
  2. Streaming before history. Persisting a batch-render message shape means rewriting persistence when streaming lands.
  3. Coverage before cartography. No garde map below 40/58 wilayas; no heatmap on a broken submit endpoint.
  4. Contrast before palettes. The existing twenty pass AA in both themes before palette twenty-one exists; severity stays locked forever.
  5. Accounts before anything that syncs, bills, crowds or claims compliance.
  6. Governance before revenue. Provenance review, DPIA and the won't-do list precede the first dinar of API income.
  7. The safety triad is never traded. Dictation review (P0-05), palette-E2E (P0-06), the dose engine (P0-08) ship regardless of capacity, quarter or mood.
